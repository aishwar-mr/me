My Story

I ended up in textile engineering, which is not where most people expect a career like mine to start. That turned out to be the useful part. There was no template to follow, no obvious path from my degree to the work I wanted to do, so I built one. At VJTI I kept reaching for things outside my discipline. I took a Data Science minor that had nothing to do with my core, ran sponsorship for Technovanza with a team of 30 and raised over $5,000, spoke publicly, competed in portfolio management at IIT Madras, and took on UN agencies coordination at UNAI. None of this was strategic. I just kept finding problems I wanted to be inside of, and the pattern only became clear in retrospect: I’m a generalist at heart, and what I actually enjoy is the part where a problem is still shapeless.

That instinct has held up when the problems got real. Different domains, same shape of work: take something ambiguous, build the analysis myself rather than commissioning it, and carry it through to a decision someone can act on. I care about trajectory over compensation. I want to earn my place in work that compounds - where the second year is harder than the first because I’ve been given more, not because I’ve stayed still. I also believe in proximity: that you become the people you spend time around, and that the fastest way to get better is to be the least experienced person in a room of curious, accomplished people. Most of the choices I’ve made have been in service of getting into rooms like that.
