How I work

  1. 01

    Build the evidence myself

    I don’t commission the analysis and wait. I build the model, the tracker or the teardown, so I understand exactly what the number means before I argue from it.

    At Ninjacart I built the pricing model I then used to make the case.

  2. 02

    Attack my own conclusions

    Before I hand over a recommendation, I try to break it: what would make this wrong, and what did I miss? I’d rather report the weakness than have someone else find it.

    In my Arrowhead teardown I flagged an unexplained volume spike as a hypothesis to check, not a confirmed bug.

  3. 03

    Turn the work into a decision

    Analysis only matters if someone acts on it. I end every piece of work with a clear recommendation, an owner and a next step.

    Each teardown I write ends with a ranked list of fixes and one open question.
